Built around availability

Calendar connections support Google Calendar, personal Microsoft Outlook and selected Apple iCloud calendars. Orbit retains busy times, without event titles, descriptions or attendees.

Prepared on your own device

The calendar worker runs on your Raspberry Pi. Current availability informs focus windows; an incomplete or out-of-date calendar check pauses time-slot planning.

Optional mailbox preparation

Separate read-only consent lets Orbit sample recent personal Inbox and Sent mail, identify possible commitments and prepare reply packs with local AI. Messages are never sent automatically.

You stay in control

Calendar access is read-only in Orbit. You can pause scheduling, choose your iCloud calendars or disconnect an account from the private app.

This is the public information site. It has no sign-in form, account connection or access to the private Orbit app. Never enter passwords or calendar credentials on this website.
